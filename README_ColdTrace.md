## The mission

🎯 The Challenge
A vaccine can lose its effectiveness when exposed to temperatures outside its recommended range. During transit, a missed warning can mean compromised stock, delayed response, and uncertainty about which batches remain safe to use.
Our answer: ColdTrace.

ColdTrace connects temperature intelligence, GPS tracking, and batch-level risk monitoring in one traceable workflow—helping teams detect excursions, locate affected shipments, and flag potentially compromised stock for authorized review.

## From temperature to action

**MONITOR → DETECT → ALERT → TRACE → PROTECT**

- 🌡️ **Monitor continuously** — Capture timestamped temperature readings throughout transit.
- 🚨 **Detect excursions** — Compare readings against configurable, vaccine-specific thresholds.
- 📣 **Alert the right people** — Give logistics staff and receiving nurses timely incident context.
- 📍 **Trace the journey** — Connect GPS breadcrumbs and custody handoffs to the shipment timeline.
- 🧊 **Protect inventory** — Flag potentially affected batches for quarantine review by authorized staff.

## 🔍 From Temperature Breach to Action

Every temperature excursion tells a story. ColdTrace captures the details needed to respond.

- 🌡️ **What happened?** — Timestamped temperature readings and excursion details.
- 📍 **Where did it happen?** — GPS location history of the shipment.
- 🤝 **Who handled it?** — Recorded custody handoffs and acknowledgements.
- 🧊 **Which batch is at risk?** — Batch-level risk flags based on configured rules.
- 🛡️ **What happens next?** — Alerts and quarantine review by authorized personnel.

## Designed for the realities of transit

- **Connectivity-aware:** Buffer readings during outages and synchronize when connectivity returns.
- **Actionable alerts:** Include shipment identity, time, location, severity, and recommended next steps.
- **Traceable handoffs:** Maintain a record of transfers and acknowledgements.
- **Data-quality checks:** Surface missing, stale, or invalid readings instead of treating them as normal.
- **Accountable access:** Restrict sensitive shipment and inventory actions by role.
- **Audit-ready history:** Keep readings, alerts, handoffs, and disposition decisions connected.

## What makes ColdTrace matter

ColdTrace is designed to go beyond a temperature dashboard. It connects **sensor evidence + shipment context + accountable response**—so teams can move from noticing a problem to identifying and reviewing potentially affected stock.

## Safety and privacy by design

- **Synthetic patient data only** for all demonstrations and testing, as required by the challenge.
- Use shipment and batch identifiers wherever possible; avoid unnecessary patient-level data.
- Configure limits and disposition rules using approved, vaccine-specific guidance.
- ColdTrace supports decision-making; it does **not** independently declare vaccines safe or usable.
- Quarantine and release decisions remain with authorized personnel.

## How we measure impact

- Time from excursion to alert delivery
- Successful sensor reading capture and synchronization
- Alert acknowledgement and response time
- Completeness of custody handoff records
- Time to identify and flag potentially affected batches
- False-alert and missed-excursion rates in controlled tests
